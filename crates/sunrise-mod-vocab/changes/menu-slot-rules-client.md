# `MenuRef::slot_holds` and `slot_gives` answer on the client

Vanilla runs `AbstractContainerMenu.clicked` on the client too (`MultiPlayerGameMode.handleContainerInput`), so an item's `overrideStackedOnOther` / `overrideOtherStackedOnMe` asks the clicked `Slot`'s `mayPlace`, `getMaxStackSize` and `mayPickup` there, with the same `Slot` subclasses, and plays its sounds through the local player. An `ItemPart`'s `item_stacked_on_other` already runs during client prediction, but `MenuRef::slot_holds` and `slot_gives` were server-only, so on a client they gave `None` and a bundle-like Part could not decide its prediction.

Both now answer on both sides. On a client the local player's live menu answers as the client's own click prediction asks its slots: the menu's slot rules, `can_equip` Parts and the mods' `menu_slot_rules`, over the client's predicted slots, which include its own unconfirmed clicks until the server corrects them; the answer is the prediction's, and the server may still refuse what the client allowed. On the creative item tabs the player's `InventoryMenu` answers, the menu a `MenuRef` names there. A mod menu's handler-backed slot answers by its layout, since a client holds no handler. Another player's menu, a stale one, or one the client's prediction does not model gives `None`. The server's answers are unchanged, and no signature changes: nothing to rebuild for.
