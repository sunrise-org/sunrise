# Q-056: Whether the viewer pauses a single-player world, and what is "the screen" it remembers

**Spec section:** 9.1.3 (and 9.1.1).

**Question.** The viewer "is a screen" that remembers the screen it was opened from and returns to it "with the
container still open". In vanilla only one screen is open at a time, and a container's menu closes with its screen.
Is the viewer meant to keep the container's menu open underneath it (so the world keeps running, as container screens
do not pause it), or to pause a single-player world as most non-container screens do?

**What was observed.** Spec 9.1.1 lists only places where a container or Sunrise panel is open, and 9.1.3 requires the
container to be open again afterwards without reopening it.

**Implementer's decision:** see `guides-decisions.md`, Q-056.
